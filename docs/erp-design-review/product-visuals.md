# Moriyati ERP product illustration system

Created for the ERP task grid and product reference. Twelve newly generated identification renders replace the previous mixed photo/render placeholders. These images describe product categories; they are not manufacturing drawings, approved package engineering or actual task evidence. All weights and collection descriptions in the UI follow `dist/collections-data.js`.

The artwork uses satin white, cool silver, graphite and clear materials. Gold is reserved for gold products. Product forms carry meaning; names, weight and purity are rendered as readable HTML, not painted into small pictures. Transparent cutouts sit on a shared theme-aware neutral surface. The square task tile contains the entire product. The same asset is used for the task, process highlight, desktop thumbnail and new-task preset.

Production assets: `dist/erp/visuals/*.webp`. They retain transparency and are web-encoded at a maximum dimension of 640px. No compositional or color edits were made to the generated sources. Original PNGs remain in the generated-images output. The built-in image generation workflow was used.

Open **Settings and Activity → محصولات و کالکشن‌ها** to view all seven collections and the cast/coin product identifiers together. Older browser-local records remap only known placeholder images; uploaded photos, notes, amounts, reports and task status remain intact.
